Customizable Brightness 1.1.1, for Minecraft 26.1 to 26.3. Press B to cycle brightness (0% to 1000%+ by default).

- New: brightness above 100% now works with shader packs. With Iris, each level above 100% raises the pack's own lighting, up to full at 1000%. How strong it looks depends on the pack; tested with Complementary Reimagined and Complementary Unbound.
- Fixed: with Sodium, brightness above 100% went back to 50% after a restart.
- Other Minecraft versions stay on 1.1.0.
